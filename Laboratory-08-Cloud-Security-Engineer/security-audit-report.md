## Security Audit Report

### The Principle of Least Privilege (PoLP)
The Principle of Least Privilege is the practice of providing users, applications, and processes only the permissions required to perform their tasks. This is important in cloud environments because if an account gets compromised, limited permissions help prevent attackers from accessing sensitive resources or taking control of the entire system.

### Attack Surface
An attack surface refers to all the possible entry points that attackers could use to gain unauthorized access to a virtual machine. These include open ports, active services, installed software, and user accounts. Reducing the attack surface by removing unnecessary components and disabling unused services helps minimize potential security risks.

### Default Deny Policy
A Default Deny Policy means that all incoming or outgoing traffic is blocked unless it is specifically permitted. This approach is more secure because it prevents unknown or unauthorized connections instead of relying on a list of known threats. By allowing only trusted and necessary traffic, the system becomes less vulnerable to potential attacks.
